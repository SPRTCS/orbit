## New

- **Devices in the icon dock.** A Devices icon sits at the bottom of the icon dock. Click it and your other devices
  fan out upward; click a device and its open tabs fan out above it, ready to open here. The tabs on your iPhone and
  iPad are now a click away at every sidebar width.

## Improved

- **A calmer hover.** Icons in the icon dock no longer grow under the pointer. The one you point at gets a soft
  highlight instead, with its name beside it and the reload and close buttons on its corners.
