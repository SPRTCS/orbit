## New

- **Reload or close from the icon dock.** Hover a tab in the icon dock and a reload button appears on its top-left
  corner and a close button on its top-right one.

## Improved

- **Open tabs stay in view inside folders.** A folder in the icon dock now lists your open tabs below its own icons,
  so you don't have to go back to reach them.
- **Clicks land on the icon you see.** Near the edge of a magnified icon, the dock now picks the icon drawn under the
  pointer rather than its neighbour.
