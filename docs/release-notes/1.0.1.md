## New

- **Remove a device from sync.** Settings › Sync › Devices › Remove… takes a device you no longer use off every
  device list and out of the sidebar's Devices section, along with its open tabs. That includes a Mac's old identity
  after "Rejoin as New Device", which used to stay listed for good. A device that's still syncing shows up again the
  next time it syncs.
