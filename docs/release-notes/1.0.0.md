Orbit's first public release: a Mac browser with Arc's sidebar and Dia's polish, running on real Chromium, with no
account. It's built on Ardia by Mantra Patel, open source under Apache-2.0. Thank you, Ardia: nearly everything
that makes Orbit fast and solid started there.

## New

- **Arc's sidebar on real Chromium.** Tabs, the address field and your pinned sites live in one sidebar beside the
  page, with Chrome's own engine underneath, so sites, extensions and DevTools behave as they do in Chrome.
- **Glass themes.** The sidebar is glass by default: each space gets a soft gradient in its own colour that lets the
  desktop show through. Settings › Profiles › Theme builds your own, and Settings › Appearance › Sidebar Style
  switches to the classic look.
- **Spaces, pinned tabs and folders.** Keep work and home apart in spaces, each with its own pinned tabs. Pinned tabs
  sit as tiles or rows and go into folders inside folders, as in Arc.
- **Bring everything over from Arc and Dia.** The importer brings Arc's spaces, pinned tabs, folders and themes and
  Dia's open tabs, along with bookmarks, history and passwords from them and from Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
- **End-to-end encrypted sync through iCloud.** Your tabs, pins and settings sync between your Macs through iCloud
  Drive, encrypted on the Mac before they leave it, with no Orbit server and no account. The new Orbit for iPhone and
  iPad syncs with them the same way.
- **Your other devices in the sidebar.** A Devices section above the sidebar's footer lists your other Macs, iPhones
  and iPads and the tabs they have open; click one to open it here. Settings › Sync › "Show devices in sidebar" hides
  it.
- **The `orbit` command.** Drive Orbit from Terminal or an AI agent: open and read tabs, run scripts on pages and
  test your own userscripts. Settings › Advanced installs it.

## Not yet

- **Passkeys from iCloud Keychain.** Apple hasn't granted Orbit the browser passkey entitlement yet, so Orbit
  can't use the passkeys in your iCloud Keychain.
- **Netflix and other DRM video.** Widevine needs Google's signing, which Orbit doesn't have.
