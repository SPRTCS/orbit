## Improved

- **The icon dock appears while you drag.** Dragging the sidebar narrow or wide now fades between the sidebar and
  the icon dock as you go, instead of switching only when you let go, and the width springs into place on release.
- **The window buttons fit.** The icon dock is a little wider, so the three window buttons sit on it.
- **Pins stay in view.** Your pinned tabs are listed right at the top of the dock instead of behind a Pinned folder,
  and a folder's back button is now at the top.
