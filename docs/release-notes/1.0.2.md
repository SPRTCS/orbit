## Fixed

- **Pinned folders unfold instead of popping open.** Opening a folder slides its tabs in from the top in about a
  quarter of a second, and closing folds them back up. A folder that started closed used to show its tabs all at
  once the first time, and the tab you're on now stays put while the rest unfold around it.
