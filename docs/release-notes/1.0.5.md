## New

- **The sidebar folds into a Dock.** Drag the sidebar narrow and it snaps to a column of bigger icons, the way the
  iPad version does: Pinned, your folders, then your open tabs. Pinned and each folder open as their own page, with a
  back button at the bottom. Hover and the icons swell like the macOS Dock, with the page's title beside them; click
  to open, scroll for more. Drag it wider and the sidebar is back.
