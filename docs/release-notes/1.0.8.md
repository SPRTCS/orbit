## Improved

- **A cleaner icon dock.** Site icons stand on their own and a little larger. Only the tab you're on sits on a tile,
  so you can tell at a glance where you are. Folder icons are centred too.
- **New Tab where the tabs are.** The "+" is now the first item below the line, right above your open tabs, also
  inside folders.
- **Your devices and spaces from the dock.** The icon dock's footer has your space badge: click it for the tabs open
  on your other devices, your spaces, new windows, history and settings. Downloads shows up there while you have some.
